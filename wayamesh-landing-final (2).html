FAQ

Your money is safe. Cryptographically secure. You control everything.

Is my money safe without internet?
Your private keys stay on your phone. Transactions are cryptographically signed locally. Even if intercepted, no one can access your funds without your private key.
Do you have access to my money?
No. Wayamesh is self-custodial. Your private keys stay on your phone only. Only you control your funds. This is true peer-to-peer finance.
What if I lose my phone?
Your funds are on the blockchain. Recover your wallet on a new phone using your seed phrase (12-24 words). Always backup your seed phrase in a safe place.
How do agents work without internet?
Agents run locally as a lightweight AI model. They validate, manage, and route all offline. When you connect, they sync with the blockchain.
Can governments block Wayamesh?
They can't block the blockchain. The technology is open-source and peer-to-peer. The mesh network is just Bluetooth—impossible to ban universally.
